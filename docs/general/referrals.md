# Referral Program

Existing Inferno Collection customers can earn $10 USD store credit by referring friends. Your friend receives 10% off an eligible product, and you receive a gift card after their qualifying purchase is complete.

## Get Your Referral Link

1. Open the [Referral Portal](https://store.inferno-collection.com/referrals).
2. Sign in with your Cfx.re account.
3. If you are eligible, copy your personal referral link and share it with a friend.

To participate, you must have completed at least one paid purchase from Inferno Collection. A paid purchase is one where the amount paid was greater than zero.

## For Referred Customers

Your friend should open your referral link and sign in with their own Cfx.re account. They can select one eligible product and receive a coupon for 10% off.

Referral discounts are for new customers only. A new customer is someone who has never completed a paid Inferno Collection purchase; $0 purchases do not make someone ineligible. Referral coupons are tied to the recipient's Cfx.re account and selected product, can only be redeemed once, and expire three months after issue.

## Referral Rewards

Once your friend completes their qualifying paid purchase with the referral coupon, a $10 USD Inferno Collection gift card becomes available in the Referral Portal. Sign in with the Cfx.re account that owns the referral link to claim it.

Gift-card codes are shown only once. Save the code somewhere secure before closing the page. If you lose a code before you can save it, please contact support.

## Important Rules

- Do not use your own referral link.
- Do not sell, transfer, or use referral links or coupons for commercial purposes.
- We may withhold or remove referral participation, discounts, or rewards where we reasonably suspect fraud, self-referral, duplicate accounts, or other abuse.

For the full program terms, see the [Terms of Use](https://store.inferno-collection.com/terms-of-use) and [Privacy Policy](https://store.inferno-collection.com/privacy-policy).
